With 15+ years of experience spanning banking, insurance, healthcare, and F&B, Jatin Gulati is a Director-level leader at Virtusa who has built a reputation for delivering transformative automation and AI programs that generate real enterprise value — not just on paper, but on the P&L.

He has secured the $33M OIA Vendor Consolidation deal — the largest automation deal in Virtusa history for a single account — and built an AI Center of Excellence that is now a replicable GTM model for other Virtusa accounts.
